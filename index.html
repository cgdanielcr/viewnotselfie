These are not current live counts, and the periods and counting methods differ. They measure visits/accesses to the areas—not unique individuals, overnight stays, or how long anyone stayed. The 45% day-visitor figure comes from a survey across 23 Dolomites locations, not a census of every visitor or a measure of spending. It should not be read as meaning day visitors contribute nothing to the local economy.
